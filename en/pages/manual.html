Abstract

The Wild and Exotic Animal Restraint Techniques platform is an online space that brings together educational content focused on teaching safe physical restraint methods used in veterinary practice. Through an illustrated guide and demonstration videos, the project presents different handling methods, highlighting the necessary precautions and the possible risks involved. The platform enables students and professionals to follow procedures clearly, observe the details of the techniques, and review the content whenever necessary. In this way, learning becomes more accessible, practical, and comfortable, contributing to the development of handling skills while promoting greater safety for professionals and animal welfare.
