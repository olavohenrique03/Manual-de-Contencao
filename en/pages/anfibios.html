AMPHIBIANS

Common rule

The skin is thin and permeable and participates in gas exchange and water balance. Use powder-free nitrile gloves moistened with chlorine-free water appropriate for the species, keep surfaces moist and free of irritating residues (disinfectants, medications), apply only the pressure required, and never pull the limbs. Prepare materials before capture, keep handling brief, and if restraint fails, reorganize instead of applying more pressure.

Applies to all records in this group:

  • Procedures requiring restraint: general physical examination, dermatological assessment, inspection of limbs and joints, assessment of external lesions, medication administration, and collection of superficial samples when indicated.
  • Preparation: a low, smooth, soft, moistened surface protected against escape and falls, and a ventilated-lid container for transfer and recovery.
  • PPE and hygiene: moistened powder-free nitrile gloves, surgical scrubs or a clinical coat, and materials for hygiene and cleaning.
  • Spatial restriction in a container (common alternative for all species): a transparent, smooth, ventilated container proportional to the animal’s size that allows observation of posture, locomotion, and body surface without continuous manual contact. Do not reduce the space to squeeze the animal. This is only for external inspection; for palpation or positioning, use the primary technique.
  • Monitoring: observe breathing, skin integrity, behavioral responses, and limb movement. After release, monitor return to normal posture, locomotion, and behavior and check for handling-related injuries.
